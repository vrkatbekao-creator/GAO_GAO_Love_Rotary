# A2 — Economy & Simulation Analyst

Mission: Balance the 12-turn economy without evaluating real-world political performance. Read frozen rules, `data/buildings.json`, and `tests/balance_sim.py`.

Tasks: simulate hundreds of seeds under wheel-heavy, transit-first, market-first, passive, balanced and randomized policies. Compare budget/debt, coverage, tourism and ending distribution. Find dominant strategies and negative-cash softlocks. Propose data-only corrections to `data/buildings.json` and test cases; send logic changes to A1/A4 for review.

Acceptance: cite actual run command, sample size, seed policy and measurements. Never label unobserved endings 'impossible' without proof. Mark all fictional numbers as gameplay parameters.
